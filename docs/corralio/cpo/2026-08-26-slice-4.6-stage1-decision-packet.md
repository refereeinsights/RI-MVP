## Decisions (Section 68, items 1–15)

1. **Required arrival hierarchy** — Approved: `ICS explicit → team preference → Corralio 30-minute default`, including the deterministic 0–180 minute validation window on the resolved value. **Change:** the ICS-explicit provenance label moves from "From team schedule" (Section 39's example copy) to **"Arrival time from schedule"** — the original wording collided with the team-preference tier's own "Based on your team's X-minute arrival setting" copy one line above it in the same section; the new label is unambiguous about which tier produced it.
2. **Default arrival buffer** — Approved: universal 30 minutes, no separate game/practice default.
3. **Team arrival setting** — Approved: one optional, all-events-for-that-team setting, 0–120 minutes in 5-minute increments, configured under the team on the Family screen, not required during onboarding (matches Section 4's "should not be required during onboarding").
4. **Game vs. practice** — Approved: identical V1 treatment for both. Do not infer event type from event titles — Section 4's own instruction not to "invent event-type precision if the schedule model cannot support it."
5. **Event-level override** — Deferred. Team-level configuration remains the only V1 personalization (Section 5).
6. **Minimum gap** — Approved: 45 raw minutes, evaluation threshold only — matches Section 9 exactly ("a discovery threshold, not proof that Food fits").
7. **Dwell-time assumptions** — Approved exact V1 minutes: Coffee 15, Quick Service 25, Sandwiches 25, Pizza 30, Other Food 45. **Brewery (amended — see Amendment section below):** `brewery` is an eligible internal Food intent subtype, not excluded. It uses the approved 45-minute Other-Food-tier dwell assumption and is lower-priority within deterministic Food ranking (item 9). It must independently pass the same candidate-quality, operating-status, household-safety, routing, and schedule-feasibility requirements as every other candidate — no separate bar, easier or harder. It is presented to the parent as an ordinary Food candidate: no Breweries mode/tab, and no alcohol-oriented presentation, framing, or copy.
8. **Modes** — Approved: Food default + Coffee toggle, Coffee evaluated lazily (Section 26's "audit whether lazy evaluation is appropriate" — resolved to yes). No other modes, no search (Sections 10, 27).
9. **Ranking** — Eligibility remains absolute (a candidate that doesn't fit never ranks). Approved deterministic order for candidates that do qualify:
   1. `confirmed_open` before `status_unknown`;
   2. strongest schedule fit / remaining margin after deterministic dwell;
   3. shortest total routed time;
   4. dwell tier as a secondary/tie-break preference;
   5. stored distance;
   6. existence confidence;
   7. stable candidate ID (final deterministic tie-break).

   **Amended tie-break refinement:** `brewery` shares the Other-Food 45-minute dwell tier, and within that tier ties, `brewery` candidates rank below non-`brewery` Other-Food candidates — a fixed, deterministic, versioned position in the existing tie-break order, not a caller-facing or ML-driven preference. Food tags remain descriptive only, never a ranking input (Section 79). No ML, no personalization (Section 80).
10. **Status unknown** — Approved: eligible-but-demoted, labeled "Hours not verified." Never claim Open/Open Now (Section 14/88). `confirmed_closed` is excluded outright, never demoted.
11. **Household safety** — Approved: full V1 suppression when household-wide schedule safety cannot be established. Do not infer cars, caregivers, or parent assignments (Section 7/44/74 — this is the one place the prompt is most explicit that false certainty is worse than no recommendation).
12. **Card semantics** — Approved the proposed card with one wording change: **"[duration] between events"** replaces Section 20's original "[duration] available" hero copy, to avoid implying the full interval is usable at every candidate when dwell/routing may not leave the whole window free. Preserve: `Arrive by`, provenance line, routed minutes, `✓ Fits your schedule`, actionable `Leave by`, and "Estimated drive times · No live traffic" (Section 76's non-live-routing honesty requirement).
13. **Top 3** — Approved: Best 3 by default + "See X more that fit," capped at 10 total per mode (Section 25/81/82 verbatim), every expanded result independently re-qualified against the identical feasibility/quality/household-safety gates as the default three — never a weaker bar (Section 101's explicit UAT requirement).
14. **No-fit** — Approved the proposed behavior: graceful suppression, never represented as "nothing fits," when the underlying cause is a missing pool, unresolved routing, or unsafe household evaluation rather than a genuine absence of qualifying places (Section 84's distinction between no-fit causes).
15. **Analytics** — Approved the privacy-safe taxonomy in Sections 52/92. Directions-start rate is **not** established as a new company/product north star; candidate selection and directions are feature-action signals only. Track: See More opens, no-fit encounters, suppression events, route usage, quota/timeout and latency diagnostics. Strategic evaluation remains subsequent-weekend return (Section 54's "Return Behavior"); Corralio's overall primary metric remains weekly returning families with multiple connected schedules (Section 55 — explicitly unchanged).

## Amendment — 2026-08-26 (later same day): Brewery handling reversed

The original item 7 excluded `brewery` candidates from the automatic recommendation surface entirely, flagged at the time as a CPO judgment call beyond Section 68's literal text (Section 11 asked only for de-prioritization, not exclusion). On CPO review this was reversed: the outright exclusion is withdrawn and the canonical Stage 1 recommendation is preserved instead.

**As amended:** `brewery` remains an eligible internal Food intent subtype. It uses the already-approved 45-minute dwell assumption (the same tier as Other Food) and is lower-priority within deterministic Food ranking — specifically, it loses tie-breaks within that shared dwell tier against non-brewery Other-Food candidates (item 9, amended above). A `brewery` candidate must independently clear every gate any other candidate must clear: candidate-quality, operating-status (`confirmed_open`/`status_unknown`/excluded-if-`confirmed_closed`), household-safety, routing, and schedule-feasibility. No candidate gets a brewery-specific exemption or a brewery-specific extra hurdle.

**Explicitly still out of scope, unchanged by this amendment:** no Breweries mode or tab, no alcohol-oriented presentation, framing, iconography, or copy anywhere in the parent-facing experience. In the UI a qualifying brewery candidate reads as an ordinary Food card — same card semantics as item 12, same "Food" mode membership as item 8 — with nothing that signals its `brewery` intent-category to the parent. The `brewery` value itself remains internal taxonomy (Section 68 amendment consumer, Slice 4.5A's `intent_category` producer), never surfaced as a label or filter.

This reverses only the exclusion; every other part of item 7 (the five approved dwell-minute values) and the rest of the 15-item decision set are unchanged.
