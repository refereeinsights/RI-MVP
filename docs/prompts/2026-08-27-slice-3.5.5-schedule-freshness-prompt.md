## Product standard

**Corralio should minimize schedule staleness through bounded polling, allow the parent to force a check when needed, and clearly disclose when the source was last successfully refreshed. Corralio cannot control when an upstream platform publishes changes to its ICS feed.**

Completion question:

> **If a connected calendar feed contains a schedule change, will Corralio detect it within the next bounded refresh window or when the parent manually refreshes—and will the parent understand when Corralio last successfully checked that source?**

The three load-bearing deliverables are faster bounded polling, a household-safe manual check, and honest successful-refresh disclosure. Do not add webhooks, direct platform APIs/OAuth, notifications, Mapbox traffic routing, new venue architecture, or new ingestion parsing behavior.
